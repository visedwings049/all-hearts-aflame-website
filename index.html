Meet Jolie

Jolie was born in New York City, but grew up in Southern California – where she has many fond memories of snow skiing, hiking and camping. In 2002, she graduated Cum Laude from San Francisco State University and began a teaching career.

Jolie met her Messiah Yeshua, Jesus, when she was 18 years old, and can ardently testify that God has been with her on every mountain top, and through even the darkest of valleys. Jolie and her family live in the Midwest. Along with being a wife and mother, she is a woman of God and a prophetic author and minister.

She has spent years studying the Bible and is continually amazed at how pertinent and applicable it still is for today. Jolie has been writing for many years, and still is today. She is very excited about her most recent book: America: the Sleeping Giant and Her Slumbering Church. She is convinced that – for everyone who has thought: What has happened to America?; for everyone who loves the U.S. and is deeply concerned about the direction the country is going; for everyone who feels like something must be done, but they don’t know what or how: this book is a must read!

Jolie has been the recipient of supernatural healings, deliverance and miracles. Her heart has been deeply healed by the immeasurable love of God, and she loves to impart these blessings to others through ministry. Many times, she has had the honor to prophetically teach and preach on God’s holy Word from behind the pulpit. While combining boldness, genuine transparency and fiery passion for God and His Word, she has been blessed to see many lives changed.

America: The Sleeping Giant and Her Slumbering Church

Voted 2026 Non-Fiction Book of the Year in the AWSA (Advanced Writers and Speakers Association) Golden Scroll Awards!

America is seething with infection. And worse than that, most of America’s church is still in bed, snoring, with the blanket pulled over her head—choosing to slumber instead of arousing from sleep and tackling grievous problems and multiple areas of spiritual impoverishment.

Thousands of years ago, God selected and set apart Abraham and his descendants to begin a whole new group of people—the Israelites. In a similar fashion, God chose and divinely inspired a small group of people to bravely travel to a new land and begin a whole new group of people—Americans. Examining multiple prophetic comparisons between ancient Israel and America, we will see that despite all the blessings and love God showered upon America, since her founding, this nation is now a far cry from its God-fearing origins.

With this in mind, we will discuss, with transparency, what the attitude and approach should be—for each American believer, and every American. Jolie’s new book will undoubtedly open your eyes to America’s true condition; as well as inspire you, encourage you, draw you closer to God, and awaken you to God’s love and your God-given destiny.

Daily Armor: Truth and Prayers

Jolie’s devotional encourages readers to go deeper, higher, and further with God than ever before. Beyond God’s mercy, grace, and unfathomable love for people, it examines the idea of whether or not they show God their love for Him by their choices. This one-year daily devotional equips believers to understand who they are in Jesus and to know the authority they have in Christ to overcome the devil. This knowledge then enables readers to become mighty, fearless warriors in God’s army! Jolie’s book dares you to participate in consistent, honest self-analyses and check their thoughts, words, and actions in light of the Bible from Genesis to Revelation. It is frank, transparent, and passionate. It covers topics and answers questions that most other devotionals don’t address.
