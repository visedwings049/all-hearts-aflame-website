- text: "Many people in America are quite disappointed with the results of the elections we just had. But we must remember: God is not a Democrat, nor is He a Republican. And He is not an American either.\n\n*Let every soul be subject to the governing authorities. For there is no authority except from God, and the authorities that exist are appointed by God* (Romans 13:1).\n\nAll the people who won in this recent election – were allowed to win by God. It says in this Romans verse right here that the authorities were appointed, meaning selected, by God. Some may ask, why on earth would God allow such ungodly people to win in an election?\n\nBut remember, God does not choose sides per say. He allows things to further His agenda. Yahweh still cares very deeply for the American people. He is well aware of the condition of our nation. And I think that it exactly why He allowed this to happen.\nIf we, as the Church, stay comfortable, at ease, we are less prone to pray, repent, and cry out to God\
    \ for mercy and divine intervention. I discuss this extensively in my book, *America: The Sleeping Giant and Her Slumbering Church*. The message of this book is extremely on time for what is happening in our nation right now!\n\n*‘For My thoughts are not your thoughts, Nor are your ways My ways,’ says the Lord. ‘For as the heavens are higher than the earth, So are My ways higher than your ways, And My thoughts than your thoughts’* (Isaiah 55:8-9).\n\nWe will not always understand why God does certain things the way He does. But that’s part of what makes Him God. He is sovereign, all-knowing, and all-powerful.  As I discuss in my book, God loves the people of this nation, and every nation, far too much to sit back and idly watch us self-destruct. So, He will allow circumstances to even get very bad, if necessary, to try to wake us up. \n\nMany politicians just won who have very pro-abortion, nonbiblical, antichrist beliefs and morals. So, what do we do now? Now that we are facing the\
    \ reality that our nation may start to further decline or go backwards from recent progress achieved? We humble ourselves and diligently seek God. We ask Him to search our hearts and lead us into repentance in all areas necessary. We cry out on behalf of ourselves, our homes, our families, our schools, our cities, etc.\n\nGod is on the throne and still in control. We need to set our focus squarely on Him. And seek to grow in our relationships with Jesus so we can see things through His eyes and hear news and current happenings with His ears.\n\n**A Prayer:**\n\nHeavenly Father, many of us can honestly admit that the election results are highly disturbing, to say the least. But we trust You, regardless. You are on the throne, and we know that You know what You are doing. As we look ahead and ponder our futures, may we not be fearful, but keep our eyes focused on You, and not all the what-if’s. We still pray and believe for revival to sweep through our nation. And we thank You in advance!\
    \ We love You Jesus, amen."
