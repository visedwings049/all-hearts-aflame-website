- text: "If you’re being honest, have you ever struggled to obey God, because it would be easier to not do so – to keep the peace with people around you, or avoid friction? I can admit I have.\nIn the book of 2 Kings, there’s a story about this very issue.\n\n“Then King Ahaz went to Damascus to meet Tiglath-Pileser king of Assyria. He saw an altar in Damascus and sent to Uriah the priest a sketch of the altar, with detailed plans for its construction” (2 Kings. 16:10). \n\nUrijah was the priest of Judah. And he was called to represent and worship God the Most High. King Ahaz of Judah, in the meantime, traveled to Damascus and saw a pagan altar there. It was an altar dedicated to worshiping false gods. Idols. The king of Judah loved it so much that he wanted Urijah the priest of Judah to construct an exact replica of the altar. He made it clear that from then on, he expected all offerings and sacrifices to be made on that pagan altar. \nAnd then it says in verse 16, “And Uriah the priest\
    \ did just as King Ahaz had ordered.”\n\nIn other words, by obeying the king’s order, as God’s priest, he was being a man-pleaser here. What should Urijah have done? He should have bravely told the king, “I can’t do that. Because that is doing directly against God’s laws. That is blasphemy, that’s idolatry. You know God commands us to worship Him alone!” But he didn’t. He got weak. And he folded. He was probably afraid of repercussions. Maybe he would have been executed for going against the king’s command.  But what does the Bible teach us? That we should always obey God over people – when necessary. Uriah the priest showed that he had more fear of man, than of God."

- text: "God expects us to obey Him – always. Yes, we should honor and obey our government. But if any person, no matter what place of authority they’re in – or any government – asks us to obey them, and in doing that we would be violating the Scriptures, then we must choose to obey God – no matter the consequences. Look at Daniel, and how he handled himself in the book of Daniel. He always chose to put God first, and God blessed him, honored him and granted him great favor for that! God always gives favor and honor to those who choose to obey Him – regardless of repercussions. He does not honor, He does not bless when people fold and compromise the word of God to honor people over Him. So, may we choose to obey God first. We must get that solidly down within our spirits and make that decision now. And decide that we will do it no matter what – so that when the tests come, we are prepared to obey God – come what may. God’s Word tells us, “Fear not, for I am with you” (Isaiah 41:10). God\
    \ is with us. We must obey and trust Him. We are in His hands. He loves us, He is with us.\n\n\n**A Prayer:**\nHeavenly Father, please forgive us for anytime we have chosen to please, or comply with people’s wishes and expectations – over Yours. We confess it as sin and ask You to deliver us of all fear of man. May we be brave and choose to obey You – no matter what the consequences may be.
